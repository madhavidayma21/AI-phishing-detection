Problem Statement & Motivation

!
THE CHALLENGE

Phishing links trick users into visiting fake websites and stealing personal information like passwords and banking details. Traditional methods may fail to detect new phishing links. Therefore, we need an AI-based system that can identify phishing URLs and help users browse safely.

✓Our goal: identify suspicious URL signals and explain the verdict clearly.

Objectives & Scope

02

Evaluate models

FastAPI service, web dashboard, Chrome extension, model evaluation, explanations, and offline mutation testing.
